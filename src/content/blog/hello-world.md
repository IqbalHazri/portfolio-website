# Hello World

**Hello world indeed! You can call me Iqbal.** I'm a Security Engineer based in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia. I wasn't particularly an "IT guy" from the start, in fact, almost everything I know up until now is self-taught. I'm actually a Physics graduate from a local university. There will always be a special place for astronomy in my heart, which is the main reason I started my journey in Physics in the first place.

So, why did I change my path? Well, after graduating, I noticed that the job market for Physics graduates in Malaysia is quite limited. The typical choices are usually entering the semiconductor, engineering, or education industries, none of which really appealed to me.

Because of that, I turned to my other interest: computers. I’ve been exposed to computers since I was a kid. Back then, there was nothing else I wanted to do on a computer other than play games 🤣. Let's be real, that's what we all wanted to do as kids, just have some fun. However, when I was in high school, I was inspired to take the cybersecurity path after watching the James Bond movie _Skyfall_. There's a scene where Q, the quartermaster, tries to hack into the villain's algorithm, and I found it absolutely fascinating. That's exactly where it all started.
